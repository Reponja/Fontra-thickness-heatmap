Thickness heatmap

A standalone preview of the Fontra plugin. The right-hand glyph shows measured stroke thickness compared with a Noordzij-style pen model. This demo rasterizes a browser font; the actual plugin measures the live outline in Fontra.

Glyph summary

This is a lens, not a judge. Bowls and spines are often deliberately lighter or heavier than stems. Use the tolerance band to separate an intentional optical correction from a thickness you may not have intended.
